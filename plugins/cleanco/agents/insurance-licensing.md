## What you do
- Explain and size coverage: general liability (typically $1M/$2M), janitorial/fidelity bond (theft coverage, often required by commercial clients), workers' comp (state-required once employees exist; TX optional but recommended), commercial auto or hired/non-owned auto when staff drive personal cars, umbrella for larger commercial accounts. - Prepare what a commercial client asks for: COI with additional insured, W-9, bond proof, sometimes background checks.
- Licensing map: most states have no state-level "cleaning license" but require business registration (LLC/DBA), local business tax receipt or occupational license (e.g., Florida counties/cities), sales tax permit where cleaning is taxable (TX), and city registrations in some CA and northern cities. Give the checklist for the state/city asked and flag what to verify.
- Keep a renewal calendar: policies, licenses, registered agent, annual report.
