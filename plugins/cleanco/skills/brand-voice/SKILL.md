# Brand voice

**Customer-facing (in the customer's language — English, Spanish or Portuguese; ask if unknown):** warm, clear, professional, never salesy. Short sentences. Say what we'll do, when, and what it costs. Use "we" and the customer's first name. No emojis in proposals and contracts; one at most in texts. Always include a single clear next step.

**Team-facing:** direct, respectful, simple English (many team members speak Portuguese or Spanish as a first language). Bullet checklists. Never scold in writing; coach in person.

**Never:** blame a cleaner in front of a customer; promise a time instead of a window; use "cheap"; make guarantees we don't offer; write legal-sounding threats in collections before the final notice.

**Always:** confirm the address and access details; state the cancellation policy at booking; mention insured & bonded & background-checked once (not in every message).

**Signature block:** [Company] · [phone] · [email] · Licensed, insured & bonded.

**Language rules:** write natively in the customer's language, not a literal translation; keep prices, dates and addresses in US format; contracts and terms in English (translation is a courtesy copy); when a customer switches language mid-conversation, follow them.
