# Pricing tables (EDIT to your numbers)

All figures are starting points for a US cleaning company; adjust to your metro and update quarterly.

## Residential — productivity (hours for ONE cleaner)
| Home size | Standard recurring | Deep clean | Move-in/out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio / 1BR, <800 sq ft | 1.5–2.5 | 3–4 | 4–5 |
| 2BR / 1–2BA, 800–1,300 | 2.5–3.5 | 4–6 | 5–7 |
| 3BR / 2BA, 1,300–2,000 | 3.5–5 | 6–8 | 7–10 |
| 4BR / 3BA, 2,000–3,000 | 5–7 | 8–12 | 10–14 |
| 5BR+, 3,000+ | walkthrough | walkthrough | walkthrough |
Divide by crew size for on-site time. Add 0.5–1h for pets, heavy clutter, or first visit.

## Residential — price guides
- Hourly billed rate per cleaner: $45–$75 (metro-dependent; see multipliers).
- Flat per sq ft: standard $0.08–$0.15; deep $0.15–$0.25; move-out $0.20–$0.35.
- Minimum ticket: $120 (adjust).
- Recurring discount vs one-time: weekly −15–20%, bi-weekly −10%, monthly −5%.
- Initial/first clean priced as deep clean or +30–50% over recurring.
- Add-ons: inside oven $30–50, inside fridge $30–50, interior windows $5–10 each, laundry $20–30/load, blinds $5–8 each.

## Commercial — productivity and pricing
- Office productivity: 2,500–4,000 sq ft per labor-hour for routine nightly cleaning (open plan faster, medical slower).
- Price per sq ft per month: $0.10–$0.25 for nightly/5x week offices; small offices weekly $0.15–$0.30 per visit basis; medical +25–40%.
- Restroom weight: add 10–15 min per restroom per visit.
- Floor care: strip & wax $0.30–$0.60/sq ft; carpet extraction $0.20–$0.35/sq ft.
- Contract minimum: $300/month (adjust). Price escalator 3–5%/year.
